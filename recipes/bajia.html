Bajia Recipe

Ingredients

  1. Cooking oil - 250 ml
  2. Wheat flour - 250 grams
  3. Food color
  4. Clean water - 1 litre

Steps to prepare

  • Mix the flour with the water until a heavy paste is made.
  • Add cooking oil to a cooking pot.
  • Add the heavy paste in small portions to the hot cooking oil.
  • Wait until ready and scoop out
  • Serve alongside chilli or any other supplement
