Viazi Karai Recipe

Ingredients

  1. A bunch of potatoes
  2. Cooking oil - 250 ml
  3. Wheat flour - 250 grams
  4. Food color
  5. Clean water - 1 litre

Steps to prepare

  • Mix the flour with the water until a heavy paste is made.
  • Add food color.
  • Chop the potatoes and boil them.
  • Add the boiled potaoes to the colored paste.
  • Add cooking oil to a cooking pot.
  • Add the potatoes and cook them for a period of 3 to 4 minutes until ready
  • Serve alongside chilli or any other supplement
